How every check stays honest

Nobody has to trust anybody. The rules are written into the app and the payment, and they apply to everyone.

  1. Money set aside

    Before anyone moves, the pay is locked away. Once someone takes the job, nobody can pull it back.

  2. Someone nearby takes it

    They see the pay, how far it is, and exactly what to photograph before they agree.

  3. The photos are checked

    Right place, taken just now, a fresh code on paper, the right sign. Checked automatically and privately.

  4. Paid on the spot

    The moment it passes, the money lands in their wallet, and whoever asked gets the answer.

Safe for the people doing the work.

Places, not people

Jobs are for shops, sites and public places. Anything about a private home or a person is taken down and refunded.

Walk away any time

If a place feels wrong, drop the job. You'll never be asked to go anywhere you aren't let in.

Your photos stay private

Only whoever paid for the check sees them, and they're deleted after 14 days. No names or phone numbers needed.
